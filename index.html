Self Introduction

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Florida State University. I received my bachelor's degree from Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications and my master's degree from Florida International University, both in Electrical Engineering. My academic background sits at the intersection of engineering, mathematics, and machine learning. I am also proud to be a Choir member of Tallahassee Chinese Christian Church.
