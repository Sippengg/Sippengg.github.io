About me

Sipeng Chen

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Florida State University. I received my bachelor's degree from Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications and my master's degree from Florida International University, both in Electrical Engineering.

My research interests center on how machine learning represents functions and models probability distributions, especially in computer vision. I am particularly interested in generative modeling, implicit neural representations, and the mathematical ideas behind representation learning and optimization. I enjoy connecting intuition with practical experiments to understand how learning algorithms work.
